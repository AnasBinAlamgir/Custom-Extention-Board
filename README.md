# Custom Desk Table Extension Board

## Overview
A custom PVC power station built to fit into a desk side drawer. Connects to a distant wall AC plug while keeping power sockets neatly accessible at the desk.

## Measurements & Specifications

### 1. Socket/Switch Placement Board
- **Length:** 24.2 cm (9.5 in)
- **Width:** 9.2 cm (3.7 in)

### 2. Full PVC Board
- **Length:** 44 cm (17.4 in)
- **Width:** 31 cm (12 in / 1 ft)

### 3. Place in Flat Form (Table Slot)
- **Length:** 28 cm (11 in)
- **Width:** 3.5 cm (1.37 in)

### 4. Socket & Switch Spacing
- **Depth / Clearance:** ~1 cm (Almost)

---

## Features & Specs
- **Casing:** Custom-cut PVC board (lightweight, non-conductive, easy to cut/drill).
- **Power Output:** 3 x AC sockets with 3 individual power switches.
- **Wiring:** 3-meter main AC line routed discretely behind the desk.
- **Safety:** Hand-soldered high-voltage AC connections with proper terminal insulation.

## Components List
- PVC Board
- 3 x AC Sockets
- 3 x Single-throw Switches
- 3m Electrical Wire
- Soldering Lead & Paste
- Mounting Screws & Hardware
